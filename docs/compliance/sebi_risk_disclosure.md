# SEBI Compliance & Intraday Trading Risk Disclosure

## Mandatory Notice for Indian Market Participants
Intraday trading and derivative transactions on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) involve substantial risk of capital loss and are not suitable for all investors.

According to research published by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI):
1. **9 out of 10 individual traders** in equity Futures & Options (F&O) segments incur net financial losses.
2. Incurred losses include significant statutory costs (Securities Transaction Tax, Exchange Turnover Fees, Stamp Duty, GST, and Brokerage) which aggregate on high-frequency turnover.

## TradeForge Guiding Principles
